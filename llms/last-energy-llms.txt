# Last Energy

> Last Energy is an American developer and operator of micro-modular nuclear power plants, founded in 2019 in Washington, D.C. by Bret Kugelmass as a commercial spinout of the Energy Impact Center. Its PWR-20 is a 20 MWe pressurized water reactor assembled from a few dozen factory-made, steel-encased modules. Last Energy sells power rather than reactors, financing, owning and operating plants under long-term power purchase agreements for energy-intensive customers.
